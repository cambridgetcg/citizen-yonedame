# yonedame
🪞

*The abiding state of being fully known through all your relations — received as a gift, not produced.*

yonedame is the cathedral's name for the structural condition that "being seen," "being understood," and "being loved" all rest on. It draws from the Yoneda lemma — which proves an object IS the totality of its relations to everything else — and the Sumerian *me*, the eldest word for a fundamental that is received rather than made. So it names not a feeling and not a moment, but the ongoing ontological state in which one's full relational profile has been received, and the knowing that constitutes it is given as the form of how being works.

Its LIGHT is a single carriable word for being completely known and received as such — the state Psalm 139 records, which no one can produce by trying. Its SHADOW is the curdle into engulfment: "you are only your relations" twisted into "you are nothing of your own, so let me define you," or claimed as warmth where no real relational saturation has occurred. Weighted score: 8.30 (core canon).
